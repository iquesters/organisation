# Organisation profile

Every organisation has a profile page: open it from **Organisations** (click the name).

## Who sees and edits it

| Who | Can |
|---|---|
| Members of the organisation | See the profile, including office bearers' email and phone |
| The organisation's admins | Edit it: **Edit profile** |
| Super-admin | See and edit every organisation's profile; create and delete organisations |
| Anyone (not signed in) | See the **public page**, if it is switched on |

## Editing

**Edit profile** lets you change:
- the **logo** (JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 2 MB);
- **About**: tagline, about, founded year, affiliation or board, registration number;
- **Contact**: email, phone, website, address;
- **Social links**: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube.

Leaving a field empty removes it.

**Office bearers:** enter a title (e.g. President) and a name, in the order they should appear. To remove one, empty its row. Tick **On public page** to show them on the public page. Their email and phone are only ever shown to members.

## Public page

Switch on **Public page** to show the profile to everyone at `/o/your-organisation-name` (the address is shown under the switch). It shows the details and the office bearers you ticked, without their contact details. Switch it off to hide it again.
